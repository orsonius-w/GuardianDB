Guardian is set on Kaimun, a world shaped by Aether, a substance that crosses into it from another universe and is the source of all magic.

Most of the world follows Sol, the most powerful god who has ever existed, whose church rules the city of Empiros on the central continent of Acerro. A smaller faith follows Lum, once Sol's wife, who broke with him over mercy and punishment. Beyond Acerro lie a corrupt western continent rich in Aether crystals, an isolated eastern continent, the islands of the serpent-like Quatl, and an ice continent where the dragons live.

These pages describe what is known of Kaimun: its magic, its faiths, its lands and peoples, and the beings who stand above ordinary mortals.
