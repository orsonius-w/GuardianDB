## Energy

**Energy** pays for casting cards, activating Traps, drawing from your Treasure Chest and using abilities. There are ten kinds: **Neutral**, plus one for each of the nine elements.

<EnergyRow />

- **Elemental Energy** pays its own element's costs **and** Neutral costs.
- **Neutral Energy** pays **only** Neutral costs.

A cost like <img src="/images/icons/2.png" class="icon" alt="2" /><img src="/images/icons/fire.png" class="icon" alt="Fire" /> means 2 of any Energy plus 1 Fire Energy.

## Where Energy comes from

<CardRow items={[{ slug: "northern-farmer", title: "A Worker", text: "Northern Farmer makes 1 Light Energy every turn." }]} />

- **Workers.** You play one Worker per turn. A Worker generates Energy either on its own in your Active Phase, or when you activate it ([A]). Making Energy is instant: it doesn't use the Stack, and nobody can respond to it.
- **Your Guardian** makes **1 Neutral Energy** in every Active Phase.
- Some Summons, Items and Buildings make Energy too.

Workers can't be attacked and have no Life.

<Callout title="Play a Worker every turn you can" tone="tip">Energy wins long games. Workers are your economy, so play one early and often.</Callout>

## The storage cap

Energy you don't spend **carries over**, up to your **storage cap**. The cap is **the number of cards in your Treasure Chest** (5 at the start), plus whatever your **Storage** Items hold. In the End Phase, Energy above the cap is discarded.

Drawing from your Treasure Chest therefore lowers your cap.

## Mana

**Mana** is separate from Energy. Summons and Guardians that have Spells printed on them have a Mana stat. They spend that Mana to cast those Spells. Mana never pays for cards from your hand.
