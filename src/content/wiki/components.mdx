Each player brings three things to a game.

## Your Guardian

<CardRow items={[{ slug: "talas-defender-of-the-meek", title: "A Guardian", text: "Talas, Defender of the Meek. 15 Life, Attack 3." }]} />

Exactly **one Guardian card**, your champion. It starts the game in your Guardian Zone and never enters your hand, your deck or your graveyard. Guardians have 15 Life, an attack stat, and Guardian Abilities that often define a whole deck's plan.

## The Main Deck

At least **55 cards**. There is no upper limit, but a bigger deck is a less consistent one. The Main Deck holds your Summons, Spells, Traps, Items, Workers, Buildings, Terrains and Events.

- **Limit.** Every card prints how many copies a deck may hold. Stronger and rarer cards usually allow fewer copies.
- **Workers** are your Energy engine. Unlimited Workers may be included in any number.
- **Treasure cards never go in the Main Deck.** They live in the Treasure Chest.

## The Treasure Chest

A separate pile of exactly **5 cards**, kept face down beside your deck:

- At least **4 must be Treasure cards**.
- The fifth may be another Treasure or one ordinary card from your pool, your "trump card".
- A Treasure is always limited to one copy and has no rarity.

You draw from the Chest during the game by paying Energy. See [The Treasure Chest](/wiki/treasure-chest/).
