Every Summon prints a **Movement** value from 0 to 3. Moving spends the Summon's **Move token**, so a Summon moves once per turn unless a card says otherwise.

| Movement | How far |
|---|---|
| **0** | Cannot move. |
| **1** | One field, cardinal only (up, down, left, right). |
| **2** | Two cardinal fields, or one diagonal step. **The most common value.** |
| **3** | Three cardinal fields, or a diagonal step plus one more. |

**A diagonal step costs 2.** Cardinal steps cost 1.

## Rules of the road

- **Crossing the Front** is an ordinary step. You don't have to stop there.
- A Summon **cannot move through** fields occupied by enemy Summons. **Flying** Summons may pass over them, but must still end on a free field.
- **Moving does not deactivate** a Summon. A deactivated Summon can still move if it has its Move token.
- A Summon **cannot move while something is on the Stack**.
- Moving onto a field can spring an enemy **Trap** once the Summon has landed.
- Being pushed, pulled or teleported by an effect is not the Summon's own movement. It doesn't spend the Move token and doesn't trigger **Agility**.

## Summoning sickness

A Summon cannot move on the turn it enters play, unless a keyword like **Sprint** or **Zealing** lets it. See [Actions & Activation](/wiki/actions/).
