Every turn has five phases, in this order.

## 1. Active Phase

- Your deactivated cards **reactivate**, and your Summons get their action tokens back.
- Your **Workers** and your **Guardian** generate Energy. Your Guardian always makes **1 Neutral Energy**.
- Effects with a Duration tick down.
- Nothing can be played in this phase. This is also the only time you may switch your active Tactic.

## 2. Guardian Phase

Effects that trigger "in your Guardian Phase" happen here: Life Regen, Mana Regen, Poison damage and the like.

## 3. Draw Phase

Draw **1 card** from your Main Deck. If you have to draw and your deck is empty, you lose the game.

## 4. Tactic Phase

The main phase of your turn. In any order, you may:

- play **1 Worker**
- play **1 Event**
- cast as many Summons, Spells, Items, Buildings and Terrains as you can pay for
- set Traps
- move your Summons
- attack with your Summons, or with your Guardian
- use activated abilities
- draw from your Treasure Chest by paying Energy

## 5. End Phase

- "Until end of turn" effects end.
- Defense and Resistance regenerate.
- **Discard Energy** above your storage cap. See [Energy & Mana](/wiki/energy/).
- **Discard down to 7 cards** in hand.
