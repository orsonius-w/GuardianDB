Every Summon runs on **two separate systems**: a budget of **action tokens**, and an **activation state**. Keeping them apart is the key to reading card text correctly.

<ActionTokens />

## Action tokens

Every Summon has three tokens, each usable once per turn:

1. **Attack**
2. **Cast**: cast a Spell printed on the Summon, paid with its Mana
3. **Move**

Spending tokens is called **depleting**. A card that *depletes* a Summon takes away all its remaining tokens.

## Active or deactivated

Separately, a Summon is either **active** or **deactivated**. Some actions deactivate it the moment they're done:

| Action | Deactivates? | Spends a token? |
|---|---|---|
| Attack | Yes | Attack |
| Cast a Summon Spell | Yes | Cast |
| An ability with the **[A]** symbol | Yes | No |
| A Movement Ability (e.g. Switch) | Yes | No |
| Ordinary movement | **No** | Move |
| An ability paid only with Energy (no [A]) | No | No |

Anything that deactivates a Summon is called a **Full Action**.

## What each action needs

- **Attack or Cast**: the matching token **and** an active Summon.
- **[A] ability or Movement Ability**: an active Summon only.
- **Moving**: the Move token only. A deactivated Summon can still move.

So an effect that only *deactivates* stops attacks and [A] abilities but not movement. An effect that only *depletes* stops attacks, casting and movement but not [A] abilities. **Freeze** does both, and also skips the next reactivation.

## Getting actions back

- **Your Active Phase** refills every token **and** reactivates everything.
- A card that **reactivates** a Summon mid-turn only untaps it. It does **not** give back spent tokens. A Summon that already attacked and is reactivated still can't attack again, but it can use an [A] ability.

## Summoning sickness

<Callout title="The turn it arrives">A Summon enters play with **no tokens** but **active**. It can't attack, cast, move or use [A] abilities on the turn it arrives. Keywords like **Sprint** and **Zealing** are the exceptions. It's protected by [Grace](/wiki/combat/#grace) while it waits in your Guarded Zone.</Callout>

## Casting Summons

- A new Summon normally enters your **Guarded Zone**, and you may cast as many there as you can pay for.
- You may also cast a Summon into your **Battle Zone 1 or 2**, on a field orthogonally adjacent to a Summon you already control in that zone. You can do this **once per turn**.
- Summons are cast in your own Tactic Phase with the Stack empty. Summons with **Intervene** are the exception.
