**Guardian** is a two-player collectible card game that fuses tactical grid combat with deep card strategy. Each player commands a **Guardian**, a champion who never leaves play, and fights with a deck of Summons, Spells, Traps, Items, Buildings and more.

Summons take the field on a shared grid. They move, take positions, and fight in sequential battles inspired by tactical RPGs like *Fire Emblem* and *Final Fantasy Tactics*. Where your units stand matters as much as what is in your hand.

Guardian has been in development since 2010. It exists as a physical card game, and a digital client for online play is in development.

## How to win

You win the game when either of these happens:

1. **Your opponent's Guardian is reduced to 0 Life.** Every Guardian starts with **15 Life**.
2. **Your opponent has to draw a card from an empty deck.**

Some cards print alternate win conditions of their own.

## What makes Guardian different

- **Positioning matters.** Summons enter close to your Guardian and have to walk to the fight. Flanks, fast lanes and zones change what a unit can do.
- **Combat is sequential.** The attacker strikes first. A defender that survives strikes back, and a defender that dies never does. Choosing who attacks, and in which order, is the core skill.
- **Nine elements.** Light, Darkness, Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Lightning, Ice and Nature each play differently.
- **A Treasure Chest.** A five-card side pool of powerful cards that you buy into during the game.
