## Elemental Energy

Elemental Energy pays for its own element's costs and for Neutral costs. Most decks run one or two elements so their Workers can pay for everything.

## Cards of two elements

A few cards belong to two elements at once, such as Workers that can make either Energy. Some pairs have names of their own. For example, Darkness and Wind together are **Night**.
