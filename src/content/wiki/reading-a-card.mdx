Owl Griffin has 3 Life, 1 Attack, Movement 2 and **Flying**. In the gallery and in the client, every keyword shows its meaning when you point at it.

## Cost

A cost has a grey number and element symbols. The **grey number is Neutral**: any Energy can pay it. **Each element symbol** must be paid with Energy of that element. See [Energy & Mana](/wiki/energy/).
