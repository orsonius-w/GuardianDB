## Incantations and the rest

- **Incantations** are **cast**: Summons, Spells, Items, Buildings and Terrains. They go on the Stack and can be **Negated**.
- **Non-Incantations** are **played** or **set**: Workers, Events and Traps. They can't be Negated and don't use the Stack.
- **Guardians** have rules of their own.

## Guardian

<CardSide cards={[{ slug: "lucius-grace-wing-king", caption: "A Light Guardian" }]}>

Your champion. **15 Life**, one per deck, and never in your hand or deck.

- Its combat type is **Physical**, **Magical** or **Hybrid** (dual). It decides which stat the Guardian attacks with.
- A **Guardian Ability** works all game long. Lucius gives Knights and Dragons the Switch keyword.
- Most Guardians also have an activated **Ability**, which uses the Guardian's action for the turn.

[All Guardians](/gallery/?type=Guardian)

</CardSide>

## Summon

<CardSide cards={[{ slug: "agile-serval", caption: "No rules text, just stats" }]}>

Your units on the grid. Every Summon has **Life, Mana, Attack, Defense, Magic, Resistance and Movement**, and a combat type.

- Agile Serval is as simple as a Summon gets: a cost, a combat type (Close Combat), and its stats along the bottom. No rules text.
- Subtypes name a **tribe** and a **class**, such as *Elf, Archer*. Many cards care about them.
- **Heroes** are especially strong Summons. You can have only one in play: casting a new one sacrifices the old one.

See [Reading a Card](/wiki/reading-a-card/) for every part of a Summon. [All Summons](/gallery/?type=Summon)

</CardSide>

## Spell

Cast from your hand. A Spell's subtype decides when you can cast it and what happens to it afterwards.

<CardRow items={[
  { slug: "freeze-over", title: "Hex", text: "Freeze target Summon." },
  { slug: "erase-magic", title: "Quick", text: "Negate target Incantation." },
  { slug: "burning-might", title: "Enchantment", text: "Enchanted Summon gets +3A." },
  { slug: "free-for-all", title: "Rune", text: "All Summons have Clash." },

| Subtype | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Hex** | Your own Tactic Phase, empty Stack. Resolves, then goes to the graveyard. |
| **Quick** | Spell Speed 2: cast whenever you have priority, even in response. |
| **Enchantment** | Attaches to a permanent and stays with it. |
| **Rune** | Stays in play with a lasting or repeating effect. |
| **Curse** | Lasts a set Duration and affects the opponent, like [Curse of Forgetfulness](/cards/curse-of-forgetfulness/). |
| **Ritual** | A delayed effect that resolves after its Preparation Time, like [Infinite Paths](/cards/infinite-paths/). |

## Trap

<CardSide cards={[{ slug: "gallows-step", caption: "Step Trap" }, { slug: "ambushed-strike", caption: "Positionless Trap" }]}>

Set **face down for free**. You pay only when you activate it, and only if you want to.

- **Step**: covers one field and springs when an enemy Summon lands on it. Gallows Step makes that Summon's controller sacrifice it.
- **Universal**: like Step, but also catches Summons that don't "step" onto the field, such as fliers.
- **Zone**: covers a whole zone (row).
- **Positionless**: needs no field, and waits for a moment its text names. Ambushed Strike springs when an enemy Summon enters the board. You may have at most 2.
- **Permanent** Traps stay after activating, and **Multi** Traps have several activations.

Your own Summons never spring your Traps.

</CardSide>

<Callout title="Think of Traps as prevention">They punish an opponent who walks in carelessly. They won't rescue a board you've already lost.</Callout>

## Item

<CardSide cards={[{ slug: "sword-of-light", caption: "Equipment" }, { slug: "sturdy-hand-net", caption: "Artifact" }]}>

Items sit in your Extended Zone, beside the board.

- **Equipment** is cast unattached, then equipped to a Summon at Hex speed for free. Each equip spends 1 **Durability**: Sword of Light can be equipped 4 times.
- **Artifacts** give lasting or activated effects. Sturdy Hand-Net spawns a Fish token for [1] and its [A].
- **Storage** Items keep Energy beyond your storage cap. See [Energy & Mana](/wiki/energy/#storage-cards).

</CardSide>

## Worker

<CardSide cards={[{ slug: "snow-glider", caption: "[A]: Generate [Ice]." }]}>

Your Energy engine. You may play **one Worker per turn**, for free.

- Snow Glider makes 1 Ice Energy when you activate it ([A]).
- Workers have no Life, can't attack and can't be attacked.
- Some Workers belong to two elements and make either kind.

[How Energy works](/wiki/energy/)

</CardSide>

## Building

<CardSide cards={[{ slug: "goblin-siegeworks", caption: "Worker cost in the medallion" }]}>

A permanent off the grid that counts as being in your Guarded Zone.

- It costs Energy **plus a Worker cost**: that many of your Workers return to your hand as you cast it.
- Buildings have Life and Defense, no Resistance. Enemy Summons attack them from your Guarded Zone, and they never counterattack.
- Goblin Siegeworks spawns a Battering Ram token for [2][1 Fire] and its [A].

</CardSide>

## Terrain

<CardSide cards={[{ slug: "lush-forest", caption: "A Nature Terrain" }]}>

Changes the battlefield. A Terrain's **Level** says how much of the board it covers:

| Level | Area |
|---|---|
| 0 | The whole board. Only one Level 0 Terrain can exist; a new one destroys the old. |
| 1 | One whole side of the board. |
| 2 | Several zones. |
| 3 | One zone. |
| 4 | Several fields that don't form a full zone. |
| 5 | A single field. |

</CardSide>

## Event

<CardSide cards={[{ slug: "still-waters", caption: "Side A and Side B" }]}>

Free to play, **one per turn**, and never on the Stack.

- An Event is **two-sided**: one side affects you, the other your opponent. You choose which side is yours as it enters.
- Still Waters: on one side, Spells cost [1] less and Summons [1] more. The other side has the opposite effect.
- It lasts for its Duration. The player its bottom side faces may turn it around for a cost.

</CardSide>

## Treasure

<CardSide cards={[{ slug: "shyrads-verdict", caption: "A Treasure" }]}>

The cards of your [Treasure Chest](/wiki/treasure-chest/). Strong, limited to one copy, and bought with Energy during the game.

- Shyrad's Verdict destroys a Summon and refunds [2 Lightning].
- A plain Treasure like this one is cast like a Hex, but doesn't count as a Spell.

</CardSide>

## Tokens

Summons, Items and Buildings created by effects, such as the Fish of Sturdy Hand-Net. A token that would go to a graveyard or the Void simply stops existing.
