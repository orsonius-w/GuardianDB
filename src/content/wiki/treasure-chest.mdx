Your **Treasure Chest** is a face-down pile of 5 cards next to your deck. At least 4 of them are Treasures. You can't draw it by normal means. You **buy** your way in.

## Drawing from the Chest

During your Tactic Phase, you may draw the top card of your Chest by paying Energy equal to **the number of cards currently in it**:

- the first draw costs <img src="/images/icons/5.png" class="icon" alt="5" />
- the next costs <img src="/images/icons/4.png" class="icon" alt="4" />, and so on

Drawing from the Chest doesn't use the Stack. Some cards make Chest draws cheaper or free. A card that simply says "draw a card from your Treasure Chest" draws it for free.

## The trade-off

<ChestTrack />

<Callout title="Don't rush every Treasure" tone="tip">Your Energy **storage cap** equals the number of cards in your Chest. Every Treasure you take makes the next one cheaper, but also lowers how much Energy you can save.</Callout>

## Treasure cards

- A Treasure is always limited to **one copy** and has **no rarity**.
- Treasures come as Treasure Summons, Treasure Items, Treasure Traps and Treasure Spells, or as a plain **Treasure**. A plain Treasure is cast like a Hex but does **not** count as a Spell.
- Treasures never enter your Main Deck. If an effect would shuffle a Treasure into your deck or put it on top, it goes into your **Treasure Chest** instead.
- Some effects put cards back into the Chest, which raises both your next draw's price and your storage cap.
