The board is a grid of **5 columns and 6 rows**. Each player owns the three rows on their side. The two outermost fields of each side's front row are **fused** with the opponent's, so the board has **28 playable fields**, not 30.

## Zones

From your Guardian outward:

| Zone | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Guardian Zone** | Where your Guardian stands, behind your back row. |
| **Guarded Zone** | Your back row. New Summons enter here. Your last line of defense. |
| **Battle Zone 1** | Your middle row. |
| **Battle Zone 2** | Your front row, touching the Front. |
| **The Front** | The line between the two sides. Summons can cross it like any other step. |

An enemy Summon that reaches your Guarded Zone can attack your Guardian and your Buildings. Your Guardian, in turn, can attack enemy Summons standing in your Guarded Zone.

## The fast lanes

The outer field of Battle Zone 2 on each flank is **one shared field** for both players. A fast lane field:

- holds **one Summon**, like any field
- counts as being on **both** players' sides at once, so it is in your Battle Zone 2 and in your opponent's
- can hold **both players' Traps** at the same time

The lanes give slower Summons a quicker route around the flank, while the center is about control. Entering a fast lane is **not** yet entering enemy territory: Infiltrate triggers only when a Summon lands on one of the opponent's own fields.

## Off the grid

Only Summons stand on the grid, with rare exceptions such as Outposts. Everything else has its own zone:

- **Worker Zone**: your Workers
- **Building Zone**: your Buildings, which count as being in your Guarded Zone
- **Trap Zone**: Traps set on fields, at most 2 per zone. Two Traps may share a field.
- **Positionless Trap Zone**: up to 2 Positionless Traps
- **Extended Zone**: Items, Runes, Enchantments, Events and other cards in play that are not on the grid
- **Deck, Treasure Chest and Graveyard**
- **The Void**: cards removed from the game for good
