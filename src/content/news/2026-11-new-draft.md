Draft is one of the best ways to play Guardian: you build a deck from scratch, one card at a time, and then fight with it. As of build 1015 it looks and feels like the new Deck Builder.

## How a run goes

1. **Elements:** pick one of three groups of three elements. Every card in the run comes from those elements, with the odd Neutral card.
2. **Guardian:** choose one of the group's three Guardians.
3. **40 picks:** take one card out of nine each time; later out of six, and at the end out of three.
4. **5 Treasures:** one out of three each, for your Treasure Chest.
5. **Your deck:** your picks plus the group's free Workers.
6. **The fights:** play decks the computer drafted the same way, until six wins or three losses.

## Your deck grows beside every pick

From the very first pick, your drafted deck sits on the right, with its cost curve and every card listed by type, just like in the Deck Builder. You always see what your deck still needs.

## The run, at a glance

Between fights you see your record as diamonds (six for wins, three for losses), every fight so far with the opponent's Guardian and the turn it ended, and the next opponent's Guardian and elements before you fight.

<figure>
  <img src="/images/news/draft-run.webp" alt="Mid-run: three wins and one loss, the fights so far, and the next opponent, Bymere" width="1600" height="900" loading="lazy" />

When the run ends, you get the whole story: the record in words, every fight, your number of runs and your best record. **Save this deck** puts your drafted deck into your own deck list, so a deck you liked can live on outside the Draft.

## Also in this build

- **Auto Pay uses your Energy Items** (Crystal Archive, Aether Crystal Cluster, Multi Tool, Stonecutter's Quarry). If a card glows as playable, it can now really be paid for, and the computer pays with them too.
- **Quartz pays automatically as a last resort**, after your Workers and other Energy Items.
- **Jong-Pun Mountain Temple and seventeen more triggers go on the Stack**, so they can be answered, among them Thornheim and Windborne Courier.
- **Earth is light brown and Wind lilac** in deck lists.
