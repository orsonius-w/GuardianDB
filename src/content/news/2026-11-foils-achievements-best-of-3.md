The last few builds of the [client](/play/) brought some of the biggest changes since the alpha went up. Here is what's new.

## Cards you can show off

Cards now lean toward your mouse. Hover a card in your hand, in the Deck Builder or in the big card view and it tilts in 3D, as if you pressed it where the pointer is. (The [gallery](/gallery/) on this site does the same.) The picture at the top shows Cinderhorn Charger at rest, leaning under the mouse, and as a rainbow foil.


**Rainbow foil.** A foil card shimmers with rainbow bands that roll as the card tilts. Foils will be rewards for achievements: there is no store and no currency, so you earn them by playing.

## Achievements and titles

There are **40 achievements** in four groups:

- **Progress:** your first win, 10 and 100 wins, online games, Draft, the campaign and tournaments.
- **Mastery:** a win with a Guardian of every element, wins with ten different Guardians, and more.
- **Challenge:** Last Stand, Untouched, Blitz, One-Turn Kill and others for the brave.
- **Ridiculous:** ten hidden ones. You find out what they are when you unlock them.

Some achievements also give you a **title**, like Veteran, Champion or Executioner. Pick the one you wear in the Stats panel of your profile.

## Best of three, with sideboards

The Bot Match screen has a new choice: **Best of 1** or **Best of 3**. It works against the computer and in hot-seat. Between games the game-over screen keeps the score and offers Next Game, and the loser of the last game goes first.

The Deck Builder has a third tab, **Sideboard**: up to 15 cards next to your deck. A card's copies in the deck and the sideboard share its limit. Before games two and three you get a swap screen: click a card to move one copy across, and you're ready when your deck is back to its own size. The swap only lasts for that match.
