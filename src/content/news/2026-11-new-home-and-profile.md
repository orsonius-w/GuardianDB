The [client](/play/) has a new look. The menus used to borrow a lot from other card games; now they are dark frosted glass with gold ornaments, the game's own icons and the Guardian logo.

## The home screen

Everything starts on one screen. On the left are the ways to play: **Solo Play** (campaign, the computer and tournaments), **Draft**, **Online Play**, the **Deck Builder** and your **Profile**. On the right you see where your story stands, with a button straight back into it, the achievement you are closest to, and what's new in this version, with a link to the [patch notes](/play/#patch-notes).

## Your profile

Click your avatar to open your profile. Pick the title you wear from the ones you have earned, then look through four tabs:

- **Overview:** your win rate, games won and lost, best win streak, fastest win, wins by Guardian element, games by mode and the cards you cast most.
- **Achievements:** how far you are in each group, which elements you have won with, and every achievement, the closest ones first.
- **Decks:** how each of your decks has done.
- **Match history:** your last 50 games, who played whom, in which mode and how many turns it took.
