The last few days were all about cards. Builds 1016 to 1029 add **143 new cards** to the Base Set, every one with its art, and the computer can play all of them. You can browse them in the [gallery](/gallery/) and grab the latest build on the [play page](/play/).

## New cards for four elements

- **Darkness** (29): Shades you can pump, a Necromancer Apprentice, the Whispering Scriptorium, Spirit Drain, and three Universal Traps.
- **Light** (32): healers, the Sacred Tabby (don't kill the kitty cat), Evil-Ward Mystic, Banner of War, Empire Stronghold, the Ballista and the Garrison.
- **Fire** (35): Goblins, the Goblin Catapult, Flame Guard, Three-Headed Dragon, Rain of Fire, and the Flashfire Trap that burns a whole row and column.
- **Water** (36): Fish, Crabs and Sharks, a family of counterspells, Clear Lake, the Arcane Forge, and the first **Treasure Building**, Merchant Harbor.

## 30 new Treasures

Five each for Darkness, Fire and Light, ten Neutral, and five more for Water. Highlights:

- **Soul of the Lich:** you don't lose at 0 Life, but every heal hurts you, and the Curse counts down to your end.
- **Divine Intervention:** a Trap that keeps your Guardian at 1 Life when it would fall.
- **Three-Headed Dragon:** anything you control that would deal 1 or 2 damage deals 3 instead.
- **Encryption Dragon:** hides a Spell in the Void and lets you cast copies of it.
- **Sword, Shield and Mask of Legends:** collect all three and a Legendary Hero shows up to wear them.

## Rule changes

- **Only "direct" damage skips Defense.** A card that just says "deals 2 damage" now meets Defense and Resistance like an attack. Twelve cards and the Burned ailment did direct damage without printing it, among them Heat Blink Garuda and Hoarding Wyrm. They now follow their text.
- **Shadow Veil** replaces Shadow Step and Shadow Cloak. At the end of your turn you may deactivate an active Summon to veil it until your next Active Phase. Your opponent's Spells and abilities can't target it, and their stronger Summons can't attack it.
- **A deck needs a full Treasure Chest to be played**, against the computer too. The Deck Builder tells you what's missing.
- **Runes no longer print "Perpetual".** A Rune is always on, so the word was redundant.
- **Soft counters ask.** Second Guess, Seasoned Wizard and Academy Professor all ask the same question now: "Pay, or it is negated?"

## Online Play, redesigned

Online Play has the look of the new menus. Host and Join sit side by side. The host picks the format (Standard, Singleton or Pauper) and gets two addresses to send, one for the same network and one for over the internet, each with a Copy button. If your router won't forward the port by itself, the game tells you what to do, remembers your external port and can look up your public IP. Choosing a deck shows only the decks that are legal in the host's format.

## In a match

- **Triggers on the Stack show the real card**, with keyword colours and only the line that triggered. The cards on the Stack are 20% bigger.
- **You can see who sprang a Trap:** "Your Trap, sprung by the opponent's Walker".
- **Shift+click** in the Deck Builder adds every copy a card's limit allows, or takes them all out.
- A new board texture, new sounds for Naga, Rats and Angels, more women's voices, and the game remembers fullscreen.
- No more freeze on a Draft fight's finishing blow, and nothing can attack a Guardian that has already fallen.
