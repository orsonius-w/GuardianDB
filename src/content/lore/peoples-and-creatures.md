## Elves

Kaimun has two distinct elf cultures.

The elves of **Thornwood**, at the northern tip of Acerro, are extremely xenophobic. They defend their forest with absolute violence against outsiders and live outside the control of Sol's society.

**Elihem** is a wood-elf homeland of a more usual kind. Its elves are far less hostile than those of Thornwood.

## The Quatl

The Quatl are a serpent-like, dragon-like people who live on Nanahuatl, an archipelago of nine tropical islands off the western continent. They dominate the human tribes of the islands, and those tribes worship them.

### Life stages

- **Hatchlings** are young, newly born Quatl, more snake-like in form.
- **Quatl** are the mature adults.
- **Ascended Quatl** have gained the power of flight and are very powerful.
- **Divine Quatl** are worshipped as gods by the human tribes the Quatl dominate.

### The Quatl gods

The Quatl practise idol worship. Each of their gods stands for one element or a pair of elements.

**Shyrad, Symbol of Thunder** (Lightning). Shyrad represents the rising star: the glow and might of youth, destined one day to die.

**Ezcoh, Symbol of Shiver** (Ice). Ezcoh represents the falling star: the shrivelling of old age and the soothing embrace that guides one to death.

**Tlazohchel, Symbol of Damnation** (Darkness). Tlazohchel represents the rotten corpse every living being is destined to become, and holds the Absolute Power of Death and the Dead.

**Acaqui, Symbol of Rigor** (Lightning and Ice). Acaqui is the Tyrant who watches over the high priests and conducts human sacrifice to Shyrad on Shyrad's behalf. The Quatl regard him as the highest god of all.

**Yolihuani, Symbol of Obsession** (Lightning and Darkness). Yolihuani is the least followed of the Quatl gods. She hides in an underground temple, plotting pain and death against the unworthy. When she emerges, her most devout followers sacrifice themselves willingly. Everyone else flees her wrath.

**Xiloxoch, Symbol of Mortality** (Darkness and Ice). Xiloxoch visits humans only when their natural time is up, and the death he brings is long and draining, a slow shrivelling into a cold husk. His closest priests are instead granted immortality through zombification.

## Dragons

The ice continent at the south pole is the home of the dragons.

Wild dragons, such as Red Dragons, were judged too dangerous to live alongside humanoids and have been eradicated from regions dominated by humans and elves. Intelligent dragons and the occasional wild straggler are left alone. On the western continent dragons persist, because it is too vast and too poorly governed for anyone to have eradicated them.

The Dragon Knights of Grace-Wing, in northern Acerro, ride domesticated dragons. These are smaller and more intelligent than wild dragons and were never the target of the eradication.
