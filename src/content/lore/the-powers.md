Power on Kaimun falls into broad tiers. The distance between tiers is large.

## The tiers

1. **Gods and ascended beings.** Sol, Lum and the Divine Quatl.
2. **Anomalies.** Beings whose power does not come from the Aether Traveler path at all. Jacob, the Nameless One and the Wandering Dead are among them.
3. **Gaufridous.** The High Paladin of Sol marks the ceiling of what devoted mortal and institutional power can reach. He is the most powerful human of the present era who is neither an anomaly nor a god, and he stands above other mortals by a wide margin.
4. **Extremely powerful mortals.** Ancient liches and high-ranking clergy and knights.
5. **Ordinary skilled mortals.**

## Jacob

Jacob looks to be in his mid-twenties but is far older. He and his sister were struck by a curse that made him immortal and turned her into a black dragon. He does not know how or why it happened. His goal is to reverse the curse and free his sister.

Jacob has three abilities.

**Immortality.** When he is killed, his body disintegrates. His sister then produces a massive egg, from which he is reborn. This can repeat without limit.

**Dark magic.** He uses illusion, manipulation and conjuration. His clothes and weapon are conjured, so he loses them each time he dies.

**Control of space.** This power has no connection to Aether or magic. He can teleport instantly to any place on the planet he has seen or visited before, regardless of distance. He can also distort space around himself, reflecting projectiles back to their source or becoming effectively impervious to blows. Teleportation does not work for his sister, so the two usually travel together, with Jacob riding her.

His sister is intelligent in the manner of a beast, not a human. She is not immortal but is extremely difficult to kill. Her raw power far exceeds Jacob's: breath attacks, claws, tail and teeth. She stands about two and a half times his height at the shoulder and is at least eight times his length. Jacob keeps her out of harm's way, acting as bait and taking the lead in fights himself.

Jacob's magic is dark, but he is not evil. He almost never kills except to defend his sister. Having lived and died so many times, he has grown detached from what he considers trivial concerns.

## The Wandering Dead

The Wandering Dead is an ancient necromancer who has lost the last of his humanity. He wanders without end and kills anything he perceives as a threat in his path. The Church of Sol knows of him and does nothing. Only Gaufridous would have the power to deal with him, and he has not.

## The Nameless One

The Nameless One was once a scribe whose magic took the form of magic ink, used to write glyphs that produced magical effects. He became obsessed with glyph-writing and began wrapping his own body in glyph-inscribed bandages, adding more and more until his physical body was gone. He now exists as an endlessly growing length of parchment, covered on every side with glyphs.

He has no special connection to Aether. He is neither an Aether Traveler nor Aether-Touched, and he has no innate anomalous talent. He became a walking god through obsessive magical practice sustained over a very long time.

The Nameless One is neutral and no longer functions as a person. His only motivation is the continued growth of his inscriptions, and he is indifferent to everything else.
