Kaimun is the world in which Guardian takes place. All magic on Kaimun comes from a single substance, Aether.

## Aether

Kaimun lies in a region where space is unstable. Tears open in it and let through matter from another universe, the Aether World, which is governed by different natural laws. This matter is called Aether. It is not bound by the laws of Kaimun's universe and is the source of every supernatural phenomenon.

Magic, in the broad sense used on Kaimun, is anything that does not arise from natural sources. It can flow freely or be deliberately controlled.

The plants and animals of Kaimun developed under heavy Aether influence, and so did its peoples.

## The three forms of Aether

**Ambient radiation.** Aether is present everywhere as an invisible radiation that suffuses all things with latent magic. Ordinary spellcasters draw on it.

**Dust.** Ambient Aether can become concentrated enough to condense into a physical substance. Consuming this dust instantly refills the user's mana and can push it past its normal limit, bringing euphoria and a sense of omnipotence. It is highly addictive and is the world's primary drug. Most of it is produced on the western continent.

**Crystals.** Over centuries, accumulated dust is compressed by geological activity into solid mineral. Aether crystals range from tiny to huge. A crystal can be ground into dust, or kept whole as a store of power. Possession alone is enough: the holder gains power rivalled only by the most talented individuals or by those who have been inside the Aether World. The power scales with the crystal's size. On the western continent, every king and dictator rules through crystal ownership.

## Magicians

A Magician converts ambient Aether into mana within their own body and casts spells with that mana. Faith casters draw their power another way, described under The Faiths.

## The Aether-Touched

People are sometimes pulled into the Aether World through tears that open at the surface. Some are later expelled back into the physical world. Time inside the Aether World does not pass as it does outside: seen from the physical world, a stay there is at once instantaneous and infinite.

A person expelled from the Aether World becomes Aether-Touched. Their body is permeated by immense reserves of Aether, and their power far exceeds that of ordinary people.

## Aether Travelers

About once in a thousand years, someone keeps the ability to pass between the physical world and the Aether World at will. Such a person is an Aether Traveler. Their access to Aether is ongoing and effectively unlimited, and their power is on a different scale from that of the Aether-Touched.

Sol and Lum, the gods of the two great churches of Acerro, were both Aether Travelers.
