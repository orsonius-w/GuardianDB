Kaimun's known lands are the central continent of Acerro, a western continent, an eastern continent, an ice continent at the south pole, an island continent in the far north, and several island groups.

## Acerro, the central continent

Acerro is vast and is divided into four quadrants.

**West.** Empiros, the world capital, and the vassal nations around it. See Empiros and the Church of Sol.

**North.** The northern quadrant is mostly untouched. The forest of Thornwood blocks overland access, so the region can be reached only by boat.

**South.** The south is very arid and dominated by desert.

**East.** The east is the least influenced by Sol. Its societies are more multicultural, and the Church of Lum does best here.

### Thornwood

Thornwood is a hostile, untamed forest at the northern tip of Acerro, outside the control of Sol's society. It is home to an extremely xenophobic elf population that defends its territory with absolute violence against outsiders.

### Grace-Wing

Grace-Wing lies in the northern parts of Acerro. It is associated with the Wind element and is the home of the Dragon Knights, who ride domesticated dragons.

## Cullian, Port of Merchants

Cullian is a sky island between Acerro and the western continent. It serves as the hub of the sky-merchants. Flying ships are an established technology on Kaimun.

## The western continent

The western continent lies mostly below the equator and has several peninsula-like arms. Its states are mostly corrupt dictatorships, alongside chaotic, anarchic zones run by pirates. Crime, corruption and violence are common and accepted.

The continent is poor but extremely rich in resources. It holds immense deposits of Aether crystals, and its rulers hold power through owning them. It is also where most Aether dust is produced.

Dragons still live here. The continent is too vast and too poorly governed for them to have been eradicated.

## Nanahuatl

Nanahuatl is an archipelago of nine tropical islands off the western continent. It is the home of the Quatl. Most humans avoid it, because the Quatl gods are too powerful for outsiders to deal with, though the Quatl mostly keep to themselves. The humans who do live there worship the Quatl as gods. See Peoples and Creatures.

## The eastern continent

The eastern continent is a separate landmass from Acerro. It is long and narrow and runs from the very far north to the very far south. Its north, centre and south each have a distinct culture.

The sea between the eastern continent and Acerro is treacherous. Flying ships are used mainly for personal travel, not trade, so the continent remains culturally and economically isolated. It follows its own religions rather than Sol or Lum.

### Daika-ue, the World's Censer

Daika-ue is a magical floating platform in the sky over the northern region of the eastern continent.

### Zishin, Academy of Visions

Zishin is an island and lagoon academy dedicated to the Water element. It also lies in the northern region of the eastern continent.

## The southern ice continent

The south pole is covered by a continent of ice. It is an ice desert, largely uninhabited, and the home of the dragons.

## The far north

A large island continent lies in the far north, with a culture of its own.

Beyond it, at the northernmost point of the world, lies a separate island. It has a temperate climate despite its latitude and is almost unreachable. Humans do not live there and generally avoid it.
