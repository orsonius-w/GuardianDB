The two great faiths of Acerro worship Sol and Lum, who were once husband and wife.

## Sol

Sol is the most powerful god who has ever existed. As an Aether Traveler he created Light magic, which heals, cleanses and eradicates. Over centuries of wielding this power, his humanity eroded as he merged further with Aether.

## Lum

Lum was also an Aether Traveler. She was born about 250 years after Sol first received his power. She met Sol before his final ascension and became his wife, and the two were worshipped together as god and goddess.

## The schism

Sol was a patriarch who believed in retribution and strictness. He rewarded loyalty, piety and strict adherence to his decrees.

Lum was open and egalitarian. She believed in helping the weak and minorities, and in forgiveness over punishment.

Neither could accept the other's beliefs. They separated, and each founded a church.

Both later ascended fully. They left their physical bodies behind, and their consciousnesses merged into Aether.

## Faith magic

Anyone who follows a god's teachings and connects with that god spiritually can cast Faith magic. It draws on the god's own power through prayer and requires no personal mana. A Faith caster receives power as a direct blessing and skips the conversion of Aether into mana that a Magician must perform.

## The Church of Sol

Solism is the dominant religion across most of the world. Its centre is the Central Church of Sol in Empiros, on the western side of the central continent, Acerro. The Church's government of Empiros and its doctrine are described under Empiros and the Church of Sol.

## The Church of Lum

The followers of Lum are called Lumites. They were pushed into the poorer east of Acerro, and Lumism grows more common the farther one travels from Empiros, especially among the poor and among women. The eastern quadrant of Acerro is where the Church of Lum does best.

The Church of Lum holds no political control over any region. The Church of Sol restricts its influence, and Lumism, being egalitarian, does not seek power.

## Angels

Angels are people who rose beyond ordinary humanity through an unusually strong connection to Light Aether. That Light Aether can come from either Sol or Lum.

In the present era Angels are essentially extinct. As the Church of Sol's grip grew, its restrictions on women grew with it, and women lost practical access to the connection Angelhood requires. The Church's own policy is the cause of the Angels dying out.
