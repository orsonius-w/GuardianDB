## Empiros

Empiros is the largest and most advanced city in the world, and it calls itself the world capital. It stands in the west of Acerro, the central continent, and is known for its splendour and grandeur.

Empiros is a theocratic city-state governed directly by the Central Church of Sol. Clerics and paladins hold political power as well as religious authority. The regions around Empiros are less prosperous and serve it as vassal nations.

## Wealth and reach

Acerro is vast. Empiros and the region immediately around it are the richest part of the continent. Wealth falls away with distance from the capital, and rural and outlying regions are poor. Port cities are the exception: trade keeps them prosperous however far they lie from Empiros.

The Church's practical control fades with distance in the same way. By the centre of the continent, little of its grip remains.

## Doctrine and policy

The Church of Sol is a strict patriarchy. Empiros fields no female soldiers.

Solism forbids drug use, including Aether dust and crystals. Its doctrine holds that they weaken a person's bond with Sol and so reduce that person's capacity for Faith magic. The raw power the crystals grant offsets this loss.

## Corruption

Most of the higher clergy of Empiros are corrupt and take part in the trade in Aether dust and crystals. Some of the supply is domestic; most is imported from the western continent.

Use is common among ordinary knights and soldiers and is an open secret at that level. Higher in the hierarchy it is kept better hidden, most of all among the highest priests.

The Church deals with genuine threats of dark magic within its territory. Its institutional priorities, however, lie elsewhere: silencing whistleblowers, preserving its own power and privileges, and subjugating heathens and Lumites.

## Gaufridous, High Paladin of Sol

Gaufridous is the High Paladin of Sol and the living embodiment of Sol's will. He is described as the most talented and spiritually pure paladin in history.

He channels Sol's power directly and commands the full range of Sol's high-tier spells. He is also a trained knight. Against towns that blaspheme against Sol, he can bring down a beam of pure solar heat a kilometre wide that incinerates everything in its path and leaves nothing behind.

Gaufridous is pious and strict, and he believes in rules and punishment. He is not corrupt himself. He knows of the corruption at every level of the Church and does nothing about it, because his priorities are extending the Church's territorial and political grip and crushing rebellion against its absolute authority.

No human of the present era is more powerful than Gaufridous, apart from the anomalies and the gods themselves. Those are described under The Powers.
